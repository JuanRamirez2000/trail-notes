This is one of my Sunday loops: an hour of climbing, a long ridge, and an easy walk back. *Sample hike: the photos are placeholders and the route is illustrative.*

## Getting to the trailhead

Bring more water than you think you need. The spring at mile 1.2 is seasonal, so check the safety note below before you rely on it. Most people finish in four to five hours, with a lunch stop at the overlook.

<RouteMap />

## The route

<Step waypoint="trailhead">
Park at the end of Cedar Creek Road. The lot fills by 9 on summer weekends; there's overflow parking on the shoulder 200 yards back.
</Step>

<Step waypoint="creek-junction">
The first mile is flat and shaded. If the creek is running high, the rocks a few yards upstream are a drier crossing.
</Step>

<Step waypoint="ridge-junction">
The climb starts here. It's steady but never steep, through open forest until the trees thin out onto rock.
</Step>

<Step waypoint="overlook">
The best lunch spot on the loop. On a clear day you can see three ranges east.

<PanoViewer waypoint="overlook" />
</Step>

## Water and bail-outs

There is no water after mile 3. Fill up at the spring first; it has a blue W on the map. If the weather turns on the ridge, the saddle road is the fastest way down.
