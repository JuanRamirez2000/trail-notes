*The route, distances, grades and pace notes on this page come from a GPS recording of the hike on April 19, 2026.*

This out-and-back starts at Red Box on the Angeles Crest Highway and has three distinct parts. A steady first-mile climb gives way to a long, nearly level stretch that wraps around the mountain to a saddle. From there, a steep and rocky climb gains about 800 ft in under a mile to the summit at about 6,140 ft. The return follows the same way: 7.3 miles and about 1,830 ft of climbing in all.

The recorded trip took about four hours, including a 25-minute break on top. Most of that time goes into the last mile: the rocky section was walked at a third to a quarter of the pace of the trail below, on the way up and on the way down.
